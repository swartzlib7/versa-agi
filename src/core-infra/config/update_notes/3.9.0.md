# 3.9.0 — Six new models, and web search by the model itself

## Tell the Primary User

- Six new models can be assigned to you and to any sub-agent: Claude Fable 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Carbon, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Sol. Each is offered with its provider's own key and through OpenRouter. They are in agitop → Models and in the model pickers once the provider has a key.
- The Claude and Gemini models among them, and GPT-6.1 Sol, always reason. Their reasoning level sets how much, and there is no "none". GPT-6 Sol can have reasoning turned off.
- Claude Fable 5.2 is the most capable and the most expensive model offered. It needs data retention switched on with the provider. Through OpenRouter that is the account's privacy setting (`https://openrouter.ai/settings/privacy`): an account that allows only zero-retention endpoints is refused for this model.
- A model can now search the web itself through its provider and list the pages it used. This is on from this update for every model that has it, and the provider charges for each search on top of tokens. They can turn it off per model in agitop → Models → Edit → Web search. Say this plainly: it is a new charge that is on unless they turn it off. It is available for Claude and Gemini models through OpenRouter, for Claude models with an Anthropic key, and for GPT-5.6 and later with an OpenAI key.
- Three faults are fixed that they may have noticed: GPT-5.6 models with an OpenAI key failed at every reasoning level except none, Claude Opus 4.8 with an Anthropic key was cut off at about 4,000 tokens per reply, and the reasoning level had no effect on Gemini 3.7 Flash with a Google key.
- They can now ask you to change a time limit, yours or a sub-agent's (30 to 240 minutes), and to explain how long a chat can last. How: the skill `versa_agi_operations_guide.md`, Live chat.
- Claude models now cost less: repeated input is billed at the provider's cached price (about 5% of the input price on Opus 5.5) from the second step of a cycle. Nothing to set. A cycle's cached tokens and cost estimate now show it, for every provider.
